Fuel-flow ringing metric: for each of six load transitions, evaluate the 2 seconds after the ramp finishes (or after the step). Compute sum(abs(diff(Wf))) minus abs(Wf_end-Wf_start), then sum over the six windows. This excess total variation measures repeated reversals rather than monotonic fuel adjustment. Units: lbm/s. Also report the largest peak-to-peak fuel-flow range among those windows. Only fully valid trajectories are included. This measures sampled fuel flow, not fuel-flow derivative or actuator wear. Results depend on the stated window and sample interval of 0.015 s.
